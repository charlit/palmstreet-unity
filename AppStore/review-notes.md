1. Purpose and audience
PalmStreet is a casual 2D skateboarding arcade game set in the French Basque Country (Anglet, Biarritz, Bayonne, Saint-Jean-de-Luz…). It is for anyone aged 4+ who likes short, skill-based runs. Value: a free, ad-free, offline game with 12 cities, a skatepark, bosses and an endless mode, celebrating the region's landscapes and culture (surf, rugby, Basque pelota).

2. How to use the app
- No account, no login, no sign-up: tap the screen to start.
- Pick the first city on the map. Tap to ollie, swipe in the air for a trick, land on rails to grind.
- Finishing a city unlocks the next one. Stars earned unlock tricks, spells and outfits (no payment).
- The endless mode opens after the 12 cities are cleared.
- Game Center sign-in is optional; it only shows leaderboards.

3. External services
- Apple Game Center (leaderboards only). Nothing else: no server, no analytics, no ads, no AI, no payment, no third-party SDK. Works fully offline. Built with the Unity engine.

4. Regional differences
None. The app works the same in every region where it is offered. It is in French only, so it is offered in French-speaking storefronts.

5. Regulated industry / third-party content
Not applicable. All graphics, music and characters are original. Place names are real towns, used only as settings; no trademarks or licensed content.

No user-generated content, no accounts (so no account deletion), no in-app purchases.
